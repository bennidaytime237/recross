Failure / Revert Tracker
Relay
—
failed + refunded / completed requests
Across
—
unfilled (expired · refunded · stuck) / decided deposits
Relay · failures by origin chain
Across · unfilled / decided by origin chain
Methodology. Relay: every request in the window with status failure or refund (both are failed requests — a refund is paid because the transfer could not complete), rated against completed requests (success + failure + refund); in-flight requests are excluded from the rate. Across: FundsDeposited events are scanned from every EVM SpokePool (addresses resolved live from the HubPool registry) and matched against FilledRelay / slow-fill events. A deposit counts once its fill deadline has passed ("decided"): matched = filled, unmatched = failed (fills are impossible after the deadline). Apparent failures are double-checked against the Across status API. Deposits whose deadline has not passed yet, or that cannot be verified, are excluded from the rate and reported separately. Non-EVM origins (Solana, Tron) are not scanned.
