Methodology. Relay: every request in the window with status failure or refund (both are failed requests — a refund is paid because the transfer could not complete), rated against completed requests (success + failure + refund); in-flight requests are excluded from the rate. Across: FundsDeposited events are enumerated from every EVM SpokePool (addresses resolved live from the HubPool registry), and a sample of the most-recent settled deposits is classified directly by the Across /deposit/status API — filled = success, expired / refunded = failed. Outcomes are never inferred from a missing fill log, so a fill the scanner didn't see can't be miscounted as a failure. Deposits too fresh to have settled, still pending, or unverifiable are excluded from the rate and reported separately. The rate is over a sample (labelled when the window is larger); non-EVM origins (Solana, Tron) aren't scanned.
